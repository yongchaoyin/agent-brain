# X growth strategy — @Gotwei / Wei AI

## Positioning
1. Strictly AI vertical, English only. Chinese AI-news accounts using the same formats get 2–3K impressions; the English audience is far bigger (format research, 2026-10-16).
2. Style: fast "COMPANY:" news, leaks/previews (testingcatalog/btibor91 style), screenshot + one-line context, hands-on tests, "why it matters" takes. Benchmarks: testingcatalog, btibor91, kimmonismus, ai_for_success, AiBreakfast, rowancheung, minchoi.
3. Pick one format and stick to it; accounts that took off narrowed positioning once, then kept one format (notjazii → AI hooks + images; victor_explore → "building ai agents in public") (0–1k research).
4. Never fabricate; verify every fact; mark rumors "unconfirmed".

## Cold-start expectations (0–1k followers)
5. Growth at 0–1k comes mainly from fast, substantive replies, not originals: 87% of shao__meng's early posts were replies; 1–4K impressions came from replies under big accounts (https://x.com/shao__meng/status/1757312998035857708). → Put most effort into replies (see x-reply).
6. Original impressions of 10–50 are normal for a new account (ziwenxu_ 25–50, victor_explore 300–700; ours 10–29). Don't change direction over it.
7. Frequency is not the lever: successful accounts post <1 to ~50/day (rowancheung 0.5/day, kimmonismus ~47/day incl. replies). Stable format + timely replies matter more.
8. Exclusive/hands-on content goes viral, reposting doesn't (legit_api's own benchmark screenshots: 260K–540K).
9. Day-0 diagnosis (2026-10-16): 0 followers, ~19 posts/day, median original impressions 7, all text-only, 13/18 with body links, generic bio, no pin → fixed by profile rebrand + image rules.

## Quota & limits (free tier) — latest wins
10. Daily cap ≈200+ posts per **rolling 24h** (originals + replies). Hit at 19:06 Beijing on 2026-10-16 after ~217 posts; an original still went through at 19:30 but the next self-reply was blocked → slots free up gradually.
11. Budget **~160/day**: ~140 replies, 7–10 originals + their source self-replies, and keep 2–3 slots for 00:00 Beijing launches. Spread across the day (peak 76/hour at 15–16h was too concentrated). *Supersedes the old "~200 replies/day" rule.*
12. Near the cap, keep the original; add its source self-reply later.
13. Original body limit: 280 chars (not Premium). Count before posting; move detail to self-reply. Premium = owner's purchase decision, not ours.

## Safety
14. On rate limit, "daily post limit", captcha, spam warning or logout: **stop immediately, don't retry**, alert Drew. After a cap, test with ~3 posts before resuming.
15. No politically sensitive topics (e.g. state AG lawsuits, Starlink/Ukraine were excluded from hot-news research). No snark/pile-ons.
16. One browser at a time on the shared box (x.com tabs crashed under memory pressure, 2026-10-16).
