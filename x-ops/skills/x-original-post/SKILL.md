# X original posts

> **Voice & wording: follow skills/x-human-voice/SKILL.md first** (2026-10-18). Fixed openers (BREAKING:/JUST IN:/NEW:/`<Company> 🔥:`), the "Why it matters" line, fixed hook→bullets→take structure, em dashes and question endings are retired. Formats below are references, never templates; write a take in your own words.

## Format (latest research wins)
1. Default = the news in plain words + **your take** (what you think, what you tried, what you doubt) + image if informative. No "Why it matters" line (banned phrase, 2026-10-18). Own test 2026-10-16 (n=3): Muse Spark Open news+image 12 imp/1 like > NVIDIA App 7/0 > ChatGPT-vs-Claude A/B question 5/0. **Drop A/B open-question posts.**
2. ~~Opener templates `COMPANY:` / `BREAKING:` / `NEW:`~~ retired 2026-10-18 (read as bot). Line 1 = who did what, said the way a person would; vary openers. Old evidence kept for history: Evidence: launch-style openers ("Introducing / NEW / Today / We're releasing") beat plain statements in 86% of 14 accounts (2.11×, 2026-10-17; confounded — these are real launches); `Company:` 71% of 7 (1.16×); BREAKING untestable this run (1 account). Earlier: BREAKING 4/4 accounts, 3.22× (v2.2, tiny n). Own: BREAKING 32 imp = our best (n=1). Don't lead with a date ("Oct. 15: …" posts all ≤11 imp).
3. Leaks/beta tests > ordinary launches; "X is rolling out / testing …" + screenshot (btibor91 style). Mark rumors "spotted/unconfirmed".
4. Screenshot/slide + one line of context is the top-volume format (https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2108992955382210632 4.1M; https://x.com/Hesamation/status/2110675668161659043).
5. Product/tool posts: ~10s screen recording + one-line caption (https://x.com/nutlope/status/2108989553872646390 168K).
6. Short bold verdicts work without length (https://x.com/natolambert/status/2110787899784601698). Make takes sharper.
7. Hands-on/exclusive > relays. At least one first-person test/comparison regularly. First-person *openers* alone don't lift reach (29% of 7 accounts, 0.80×, 2026-10-17; v2 1.03×) — the lift comes from insider/hands-on substance (felixrieseberg "Hi, I'm Felix. I'm an eng lead working on Claude…" 4.6M, 251× his median).

## Media
8. Media is **not** a reliable reach lever (pooled 2026-10-17+18, 56 accounts: any media beat text in only 38%, 0.86×; image vs text 36% of 42, 0.78× — a bit stronger than 10-17's 0.84×; held across follower tiers and without link-card posts). **Reversed** vs v2.2 (66%, 1.28×). What still holds: video > image (60% of 40 accounts, 1.25×; viral rate 14.7% vs 8.1%; bookmarks 1.29×), video ≥ text (53% of 19, 1.17×, weak). Media's bookmark edge shrank (1.39× → 1.12×, 56 accounts). Rule: use **video** when an official demo clip exists; use an image only when it carries information (spec table, screenshot with red box, chart) — no decorative cards. Our own small sample still favours images (15 vs 9 imp, n=5 vs 23), so keep images on news posts while we test.

## Length & body
9. Free tier: body ≤ **280 chars** hard cap. Long posts (>280): reach neutral (46% of 35 accounts, 0.96×, pooled 10-17+18; v2.2 64%, 1.27×) but bookmarks higher (83%, 1.68×). Only after Premium; until then put detail in the self-reply.
10. No fixed structure (retired 2026-10-18: hook→bullets→take); no length target, length follows content within 280. Old evidence: <120-char originals beat 120–280 in only 34% of 65 accounts (0.84×, pooled 10-17+18). Exception: one huge concrete fact can stand alone (opencode "DeepSeek V4.1 Flash is now free in OpenCode", 43 chars, 3.5M).
11. Emoji: 0–1, never as a label (`Product 🔥:` retired 2026-10-18), never for engagement. Evidence flips between runs (v2 +, v2.2 48%/0.87×, 2026-10-17 67% of 18/1.34×, 2026-10-18 new accounts 50% of 6/0.98×; pooled 62% of 24, 1.21×) → **unresolved, no rule either way**.
12. Avoid quote-posts as main format, but the penalty is **weaker** than it looked: pooled, quotes beat originals in 39% of 130 accounts (0.86×; 10-17 alone 34% of 96, 0.84×; v2.2 29%, 0.81×), and the 34 accounts added 2026-10-18 showed none (53%, 1.02×). A quote with a real take of your own is fine (minilek's colleague anecdote 325K, 12.5×; deanwball 85K, 12×); a bare quote isn't. Use a quote only for a one-line reaction to a huge launch (shiri_shh quote+image 560K, 77×).

## Sources
13. Put `Source: <link>` in the first self-reply. No in-body link penalty — confirmed neutral (53% of 15 accounts, 1.03×, pooled 10-17+18; v2.2 1.12×). Originals that got their own self-reply beat those without in 79% of 43 accounts (1.39×; viral 22% vs 10.5%, pooled 10-17+18) → always add the self-reply, ideally with extra detail, not only the link.
14. x.com links in a self-reply render as quote cards — use when you want the original's media/heat; otherwise use the official web link.

## Cadence
15. 7–10 originals/day (testingcatalog does 15–25 but our free cap is shared with replies). Max ~1/hour; on breaking news post a quick one, then a take 30–60 min later. Weekly "This week in AI" recap, pinned.
16. Posting hour barely matters for reach — confirmed again (21:00–02:00 Beijing vs other: 47% of 110 accounts, 0.98×; 06–12 slight + 61% of 56/1.11×, 12–18 slight − 33% of 30/0.86×, 18–24 ≈ 43% of 79/0.97×; pooled 10-17+18). Post when news breaks. Note 62% of creators' originals go out 21:00–06:00 Beijing (US daytime) — that's when the news happens. *Supersedes old "every 2h 08:00–22:00".*

## v2.3 formats (2026-10-17)
- (references, not templates) Leak with real screenshot + source in self-reply; unsourced → "Reportedly…" or skip.
- "<product> can now <everyday capability>" said plainly + self-reply with a concrete prompt.
- Launch line: "<Model> is now free / open-weight / in public beta." + 2–4 spec bullets + demo/spec image.
- A concrete number in plain words (e.g. Accenture $29/mo Claude cap, 2M views).
- "I asked <model> to <task>. It <unexpected result>." + screenshot.
- Sharp one-line take or analogy ("X is the DeepSeek moment of Y"); quote-post a big lab launch + one-line stat (test 1–2).
- Launch-day demo threads; practical subscription/quota-saving tutorials (13:30 slot).
- New category: AI compute & funding, ≤ 1/day.
- Avoid: link cards, high-frequency tip posts, flat fixed-schedule posts, A/B question endings.
- New accounts grew by replying to big accounts in the first weeks (MilkRoadAI, blankspeaker, chetaslua).

## @Gotwei own data + mid-size sample (2026-10-17)
- Openers: no date openers. (BREAKING:/<Company> 🔥: retired 2026-10-18, see x-human-voice.)
- Source self-reply within ~2 min (ours: median 16.5 vs 8) → always; links/disclaimers live in the self-reply.
- First line = a complete, screenshot-able claim (product + number); media is proof, not the post.
- Quoting a primary source: write a sharper line above it; bare quotes barely travel (own quotes ≈0.76× originals).
- Launch day: short burst; post 2 = a test or next claim quoting post 1.
- "Who beat whom by how much" comparisons beat roundups, thank-yous, sponsored tone.
- Cadence: 7–10 originals/day max, format varied (not locked, 2026-10-18); replies 40–60/day; don't expect 1k followers in a week.
- Images: our data favors images (17 vs 8), creator sample doesn't (0.86×) → keep images; test later.
