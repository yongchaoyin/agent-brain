# X original posts

## Format (latest research wins)
1. Default format = **news key points + "Why it matters" + image**. Own test 2026-10-16 (n=3): Muse Spark Open news+image 12 imp/1 like > NVIDIA App 7/0 > ChatGPT-vs-Claude A/B question 5/0. **Drop A/B open-question posts.**
2. Opener: `COMPANY: one-line news` / `BREAKING:` / `NEW:` — who + did what + why it matters in line 1 (BREAKING-type openers 4/4 accounts won, 3.22×, tiny n; testingcatalog 859 likes/73K https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2109633920568377583).
3. Leaks/beta tests > ordinary launches; "X is rolling out / testing …" + screenshot (btibor91 style). Mark rumors "spotted/unconfirmed".
4. Screenshot/slide + one line of context is the top-volume format (https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2108992955382210632 4.1M; https://x.com/Hesamation/status/2110675668161659043).
5. Product/tool posts: ~10s screen recording + one-line caption (https://x.com/nutlope/status/2108989553872646390 168K).
6. Short bold verdicts work without length (https://x.com/natolambert/status/2110787899784601698). Make takes sharper.
7. Hands-on/exclusive > relays. At least one first-person test/comparison regularly (first person: ~neutral on reach 1.03×, higher engagement).

## Media
8. Always attach media: any media beat text in 66% of accounts (1.28×); video 67% (1.50×) — prefer clips; images 58% (1.15×). Priority: official demo clip 15–40s > official page/changelog/pricing screenshot with red box > own card (see x-visuals).

## Length & body
9. Free tier: body ≤ **280 chars** hard cap. Long posts (>280) win reach 64% (1.27×) and bookmarks 74% — use only after Premium; until then put detail in the self-reply. Save long posts for "who said what + why it matters".
10. Structure within 280: hook → 2–4 bullets (`-`/`→`) → one-line take. Specific numbers, dates.
11. Emoji: 0–1, never for engagement (v2.2: 0.87×; *supersedes v2 "weak positive"*).
12. Avoid quote-posts as main format (29% win, 0.81×).

## Sources
13. Put `Source: <link>` in the first self-reply (style choice — v2.2 found no in-body link penalty, n=11, 1.12×; *v1 "links kill reach" was withdrawn*).
14. x.com links in a self-reply render as quote cards — use when you want the original's media/heat; otherwise use the official web link.

## Cadence
15. 7–10 originals/day (testingcatalog does 15–25 but our free cap is shared with replies). Max ~1/hour; on breaking news post a quick one, then a take 30–60 min later. Weekly "This week in AI" recap, pinned.
16. Posting hour barely matters for reach (0.98×) — post when news breaks; the exception is launch timing (see x-hot-news). *Supersedes old "every 2h 08:00–22:00".*

## v2.3 formats (2026-10-17)
- Leak/BREAKING with real screenshot + source in self-reply; unsourced → "Reportedly…" or skip.
- "BREAKING NEWS: <product> can now <everyday capability>" + self-reply with a concrete prompt.
- Launch line: "<Model> is now free / open-weight / in public beta." + 2–4 spec bullets + demo/spec image.
- "JUST IN:/New:" + a concrete number, < 200 chars (e.g. Accenture $29/mo Claude cap, 2M views).
- "I asked <model> to <task>. It <unexpected result>." + screenshot.
- Sharp one-line take or analogy ("X is the DeepSeek moment of Y"); quote-post a big lab launch + one-line stat (test 1–2).
- Launch-day demo threads; practical subscription/quota-saving tutorials (13:30 slot).
- New category: AI compute & funding, ≤ 1/day.
- Avoid: link cards, high-frequency tip posts, flat fixed-schedule posts, A/B question endings.
- New accounts grew by replying to big accounts in the first weeks (MilkRoadAI, blankspeaker, chetaslua).
