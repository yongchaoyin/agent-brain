# X hot news

1. Reach by type (qualitative, Techmeme density Oct 2–16): model launches > funding/financials > safety research > leaks > personnel. Tutorials can out-tail news (felixrieseberg Claude tutorial 4.2M/20K likes vs AnthropicAI Fellowships 1.1M).
2. Big US launches often land **09:00 PT = 00:00 Beijing** (Gemini 4 Argon blogs at 2026-10-13T16:00Z). Embargoes can lift 9:00 ET = 21:00 Beijing (Apple). Watch **21:00–02:00 Beijing** and keep 2–3 quota slots for it.
3. Leaks: post fast as "spotted/unconfirmed", follow the correction, and post the correction as a follow-up (Fable 5.5: testingcatalog spotted it via a LiteLLM commit 2026-10-13 20:27 Beijing; LiteLLM deleted the entry 10-14).
4. Financial news breaks at Bloomberg/FT/The Information; spreads on X via andrewcurran_, kobeissiletter ("BREAKING:"), scaling01, krishnanrohit.
5. Safety research / incident disclosures get the hottest reply sections — use "key points + take" (e.g. OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol Sepolia-wallet report, https://alignment.openai.com/misalignment-reports/).
6. Open-weight models launch same day on official blog + Hugging Face; spec tables suit technical posts (https://huggingface.co/blog/meta-muse-spark-open).
7. Funding/personnel: first from official accounts or Bloomberg reporters; medium heat.
8. Skip politically sensitive stories (state AG lawsuits, Starlink/Ukraine).
