# @Gotwei growth skill

Read `x-ops/playbook.md` in this repo before each run; it holds the full rules, templates and evidence.

## Core rules
1. Strictly AI vertical. English. Never fabricate; verify every fact.
2. No external links in the main post; put "Source: <link>" in a self-reply.
3. Prefer video, then image/screenshot; plain text last. Lists and threads earn bookmarks.
4. Hooks: "BREAKING:" / "<COMPANY> 🔥:" for news; first-person hands-on takes and opinions beat neutral summaries.
5. Replies (~200/day, spaced out): target fresh, high-engagement posts from big AI accounts (Tibo, sama, karpathy, official labs). ~50 chars, add a fact, number or sharp question. No "🔥"-only replies, no links, vary wording.
6. Original posts every 2 hours (08:00–22:00 Beijing) on the hottest AI topic; weekly recap thread, pinned.
7. Stop and alert Drew on any rate-limit, spam warning, or logout.
